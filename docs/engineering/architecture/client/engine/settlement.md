# Settlement

## 1. Introduction and Goals

The queue owns Mutation lifecycle and final typed outcome. A committed acknowledgment saves the execution result; accepted work waits for coverage through its `syncCursor` and for required Stream target evidence or permitted receipt fallback. Evidence arrives only through the Stream. A Stream target at or below S in a Model the active materialization does not Bootstrap finalizes from its receipt fallback, as a Private target does. Private fallback cannot replace newer authority or undo later independent writes. Queue retirement, Model replay and terminal notification eligibility share the settlement transaction.
